How It Works

Your body is the input layer.

• Look left / right — webcam + MediaPipe tracks head movement to scroll and navigate (runs in the browser).

• Jaw clench / brow raise — Emotiv facial signals for enter, exit, and asking the assistant (local bridge + headset).

• Voice — AI assistant with a noise-aware gate (≥50% audio catch) and 2 seconds of silence before it responds.

• Personal calibrator (in progress) — short onboarding learns your thresholds and saves your profile.

Today: face + voice + facial EMG.
Next: EEG motor imagery as a first-class input.

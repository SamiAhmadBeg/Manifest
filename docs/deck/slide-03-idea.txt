The Idea

Manifest — inexpensive, personal, plug-and-play.

• A biosignal-native OS: scroll, open apps, play games, watch movies, and talk to an AI assistant — no mouse or keyboard.

• Pretrained controls out of the box, plus a personal calibrator that learns your signals — not lab averages.

• Two markets:
  — Access for ALS and tetraplegia (hands-free computing people can actually use)
  — A sandbox for BCI headset owners (no more build-everything-from-scratch)
